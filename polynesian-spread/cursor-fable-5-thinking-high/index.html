The Wayfinders' Atlas

A runnable model of how Austronesian peoples crossed the Pacific — from the homeland off Taiwan out to the three corners of the Polynesian Triangle (Hawaiʻi, Rapa Nui, Aotearoa). The map and timeline below are built from the real record (radiocarbon, genetics, linguistics, oceanography). The simulation lays voyaging physics over that world and asks the headline question: what must the canoes and the strategy have been capable of to reproduce the spread we observe — and which assumptions fail?
