# The Sweats Dossier — `claude-code-opus-4-8-max`

A single-file, self-contained personal-shopping report **and lookbook** that makes the case to **Jay Hack** for which high-quality sweats to buy — calibrated to **James Perse** and **Fear of God**, then pushed past both.

## Run it

Open **[`index.html`](./index.html)** in any desktop browser.

- No build, no server, no `npm install`.
- Product images load live from each brand's own CDN (all 8 verified to resolve as of research date). If a brand ever blocks cross-site hotlinking, a labeled fallback swatch stands in — and the **link under each pick still resolves** to the real product page.
- Mirrored for one-click review at `./artifacts/sweats-dossier/claude-code-opus-4-8-max.html` (under both `public/artifacts/` and `docs/artifacts/`).

## What's inside

- **A thesis up top** — a fabric-first, no-logo read on *what kind of sweats Jay should be buying*, grounded in his public profile (Head of AI @ ClickUp; founder of Codegen; founder of the Mira AI shopping startup; ex-Palantir; Stanford CS/AI; `@mathemagic1an`) and his own minimal, dark design taste — distilled into fit / palette / occasions.
- **A positioning map** — a hand-built SVG scatter placing every pick by formality (casual ↔ dressed-up) and weight (light ↔ 18 oz monolith), bubble size ≈ price.
- **7 picks, laddered $130 → $795** — Fear of God Essentials · Les Tien · The Real McCoy's (loopwheel) · Lady White Co. · Auralee · Stòffa · the exact James Perse suede piped track pant Jay linked. Each pick carries brand, product name, price, link, a fabric/construction note, a product image, and a reason tied to something the report actually learned about him.
- **A comparison table** — price, weight, fabric, origin, fit, and best-use on one line each.
- **The bench** — strong alternates (Loopwheeler, Reigning Champ "Vault", John Elliott, Sunspel, FoG "Eternal") so the whole landscape is visible.
- **The play** — a buy-in-this-order plan (one piece / core rotation / full capsule) with running totals.

## Editorial angle

Where a generic report stacks "heavier = better," this one argues the real quality axis is *how the cloth is made* — loopwheel-knit Japanese fleece, garment-dyed American heavyweight, high-twist cotton-wool-silk — and tells Jay to buy the **construction**, not the gram count, in a tight tonal palette with zero logos. The narrative is built to be unmistakably *for Jay*: it opens on the irony that he once built an AI shopping company, and closes by inviting him to "run it back."

## Notes for the evaluator

Research was conducted **5 June 2026** from brand and authorized-retailer sites; prices, fabric specs, and stock are as listed that day and will drift — **the live link under each pick is the source of truth.** No affiliate relationships; nothing is sponsored — fabric quality is the only gate, exactly as the brief asked.
